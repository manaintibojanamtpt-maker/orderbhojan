# HOME Experience Brief — PX6 Phase 0

**Screen:** Home  
**Program:** Product Excellence Organization (PX6)  
**Status:** Active sprint — Restaurant, Menu, Search, Cart, Profile LOCKED  
**Authority:** Experience Director  
**Contains:** Customer experience only. No UI. No code. No layout.

---

## Customer Persona

**Name:** Priya  
**Age:** 28  
**Location:** Kondapur, Hyderabad  
**Context:** Working professional, orders dinner 4–5 nights a week  
**Relationship to food:** Food is comfort, culture, and relief after a long day — not a transaction  
**Tech comfort:** High; uses Swiggy/Zomato but tired of cluttered, noisy interfaces  
**Brand awareness:** Knows Mana Inti Bojanam as warm, trustworthy, hygienic

---

## Situation

Tuesday, 7:45 PM. Priya just closed her laptop. She has 45 minutes before a call. She has not eaten since lunch. She opens OrderBhojan because she wants **something good nearby** — not to browse an app.

---

## Emotion (arrival)

- Tired  
- Hungry (building)  
- Slightly impatient  
- Hoping for comfort, not decisions  

---

## Goal

Find and order food she will genuinely enjoy — quickly, with confidence — without feeling like she is using "another delivery app."

---

## Pain (today's alternatives)

- Too many badges, offers, and banners competing for attention  
- Generic stock photos that do not look like food she eats  
- Cluttered cards that feel like dashboards, not kitchens  
- Uncertainty: *Is this place good? Is the food fresh?*  
- Cognitive load before hunger is satisfied  

---

## Desired Feeling

| Moment | Feeling |
|--------|---------|
| Open app (0–2s) | **Craving** — stomach responds before brain reads |
| Scan (2–10s) | **Warmth** — like a trusted local guide, not software |
| Choose (10–30s) | **Confidence** — clear photos, honest ratings, simple choices |
| After order | **Anticipation** — remembers the dish, not the interface |
| Tomorrow | **Memory of food** — biryani steam, dosa crisp, thali colors |

---

## Success Criteria

1. User feels hungry within **two seconds** of opening Home (HOME RULE)  
2. User understands what OrderBhojan offers without instruction  
3. User trusts the restaurants shown (real photography, hygiene cues)  
4. User reaches a restaurant or search intent within **30 seconds**  
5. User remembers **food**, not UI, when describing the app later  
6. Experience feels **Indian** — regional warmth, not Western generic marketplace  
7. Experience feels **premium** — calm, spacious, intentional  

---

## Failure Criteria

1. User sees placeholder graphics, colored rectangles, or text-in-image before food  
2. User reads marketing copy or badges before feeling hunger  
3. User thinks *"This looks unfinished"* or *"Is this real?"*  
4. User notices cards, gradients, or navigation chrome before food  
5. User cannot distinguish OrderBhojan from generic delivery clones  
6. User abandons before selecting a restaurant due to clutter or distrust  
7. User remembers the **interface**, not the **food**  

---

## Out of scope (this brief)

Restaurant detail, menu browsing, checkout, cart animation, search results, profile — locked screens. Home exists to **create craving and route intent**.
